Privacy Policy

Your time is personal. This policy explains how OWNHOUR handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: November 15, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how OWNHOUR ("we", "us", or "the app") handles information when you use the OWNHOUR iOS application. OWNHOUR is a screen-time and digital wellbeing app that lets you choose apps, set a shared daily usage budget, review usage insights, and optionally receive reminders.

OWNHOUR does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

2. Screen Time access and app selections

When you choose to authorize Screen Time access, OWNHOUR uses Apple's FamilyControls, DeviceActivity, and ManagedSettings frameworks to provide the features you request.

  • App selections: You select the apps you want to manage using Apple's system-provided selection interface. OWNHOUR uses the privacy-preserving app and category tokens provided by Apple.
  • Usage information: The app uses Screen Time activity information for your selected apps to calculate the combined daily allowance, show relevant usage information and insights, and determine when the selected apps should be shielded.
  • Shielding: When your shared daily allowance is exhausted, OWNHOUR can ask iOS to shield the selected apps until the next daily reset.

OWNHOUR does not read the content inside other apps, such as messages, photos, passwords, or posts. It does not identify activity inside a particular screen or feature (for example, a specific feed or messaging section). The selected app is managed as a whole.

Screen Time information is used to provide OWNHOUR's screen-time features. Under the current app design, this information is processed on your device and in the app's shared on-device storage used by its app extensions; it is not uploaded to an OWNHOUR-operated server.

3. Settings, budgets, and insights

OWNHOUR may store app settings on your device, such as your selected daily budget, preferences, reminder settings, selected-app tokens, and information needed to display usage summaries. This information is used to operate the app, maintain your daily budget, and display your insights.

4. Notifications and reminders

Reminders are optional. If you enable them and grant notification permission, OWNHOUR may use iOS notification features and Screen Time activity events to remind you as you approach or reach your limit. You can change notification permission at any time in your device's Settings. Turning off notifications does not itself revoke Screen Time authorization.

5. Subscriptions and payments

OWNHOUR offers optional subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase and StoreKit services. Apple processes payments and manages billing. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. OWNHOUR uses the purchase or subscription status made available by Apple to determine whether Pro features are available.

Your purchases are also subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. You can view or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting OWNHOUR does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. OWNHOUR does not share your Screen Time activity or selected-app information with advertisers. Apple processes information as needed to provide iOS Screen Time permissions, App Store purchases, and subscription services under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process. The current app design does not require an OWNHOUR account or a developer-operated service to store your Screen Time history.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information used by OWNHOUR's core features is stored locally on your device for as long as it is needed to operate the app and show your settings or insights. You can stop future Screen Time access by revoking OWNHOUR's permission in iOS Settings. You can remove OWNHOUR's locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

Revoking Screen Time access may stop usage monitoring and shielding features from working. Apple may retain purchase, billing, or transaction records under its own policies and legal obligations. Deleting the app does not delete records held by Apple or cancel subscriptions.

8. Security

We rely on iOS's platform protections and on-device storage for the app's local information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Children's privacy

OWNHOUR is not designed to collect personal information from children, and it does not require a personal account. Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use of device-level Screen Time features and permissions.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
